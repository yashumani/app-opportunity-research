# Domain 4 — Creator / Photo / Video AI

**Recommendation:** PRIORITY 4 — pursue only with a sharply vertical workflow  
**Working concept:** Profession-specific content studio rather than a generic AI editor.

## Why this domain is attractive — and dangerous

Photo & Video is one of the strongest monetizing subscription categories, but also one of the most competitive. RevenueCat's 2026 benchmarks classify roughly **61.4% of Photo & Video apps as AI-powered**.

The viable founder strategy is to own an **end-to-end job for one profession**, such as real-estate listings, restaurant social content, marketplace seller assets, or contractor before/after packages.

## Recommended wedge

A strong candidate is:

> **AI Listing Studio for local service businesses or real-estate professionals.**

Upload a batch of photos once; receive platform-ready images, captions, story/reel assets, and a publish queue.

## Unit economics

Image processing/generation can often stay in the few-cents to low-tens-of-cents range per output, depending quality and provider. Video is the major cost risk.

Current Sora 2 reference pricing is around **$0.10/second** for 720p generation. That implies about $1 for a 10-second generation before retries. Heavy regeneration can quickly destroy a low-priced unlimited plan.

**Rule:** do not launch unlimited AI video. Use credits and transparent usage limits.

## Pricing hypothesis

- Starter: $29-$49/month.
- Professional: $79-$129/month.
- Agency: $199-$499/month.
- Additional generation credits separately.

## Validation

Do not build an editor first. Pick one profession, recruit 10-20 businesses, manually create weekly content packages using existing tools, charge $50-$150, and track whether customers actually post the assets and repurchase.

**Go signal:** at least five businesses pay for a second month/package.
