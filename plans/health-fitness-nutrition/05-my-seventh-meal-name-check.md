# Name Check — My Seventh Meal

**Screening date:** August 9, 2026  
**Candidate:** `My Seventh Meal`  
**Status:** Preliminary public screening only; not a legal-clearance opinion

## Decision

`My Seventh Meal` appears **preliminarily clear as an exact public name**, but it is **not yet confirmed available** and is not the strongest product-positioning name for a photo-based nutrition tracker.

### Current recommendation

- **Exact public collision:** No obvious exact Apple App Store, Google Play, nutrition-company, website, or indexed U.S. trademark match found.
- **Proceed to live reservation checks:** Yes, if the founder strongly prefers the name.
- **Brand recommendation:** Keep as a secondary finalist rather than the automatic winner.
- **Overall preliminary score:** **6.4 / 10**.

## What was checked

### Public app-store results

No exact public listing was found for:

- `My Seventh Meal`
- `My 7th Meal`
- `Seventh Meal`
- `7th Meal`

Public search cannot reveal private or unreleased App Store Connect records. Apple permits an app name for one app per localization; the definitive test is creating the intended app record. Apple also limits the name to 30 characters.

### Public web and business results

No obvious exact active brand or company called `My Seventh Meal` was found. Most exact-phrase results were ordinary sentences such as people describing their seventh meal of a day or trip.

However, the broader `seven meal` space is populated:

- **Mealtime7** — active Apple application for AI family meal planning.
- **Seven Meal / 7Meal** — established Japanese meal-ordering service associated with 7-Eleven, using `7meal.jp` and related `7meal` web identity.
- **7Mealz** — existing web brand.
- Multiple meal-prep and restaurant loyalty programs use language such as “the seventh meal is free.”

These are not exact matches, but they create search and meaning adjacency.

### Domains

Ordinary public search did not surface active indexed sites for:

- `myseventhmeal.com`
- `my7thmeal.com`
- `seventhmeal.com`
- `7thmeal.com`

This is **not a live registrar confirmation**. A domain may be registered, parked, premium, newly changed, or reserved without being indexed. Run an authoritative RDAP/registrar checkout and purchase the chosen domain immediately after confirming the legal owner.

### Trademark screening

No obvious indexed exact U.S. trademark match was found for `MY SEVENTH MEAL` during preliminary public search.

That is not a full clearance. Similar sound, appearance, meaning, or commercial impression can create conflict even when wording is not identical. A comprehensive search must include the USPTO database, state registrations, business records, app stores, domains, and earlier common-law internet use.

## Main positioning concern

For a typical customer, `My Seventh Meal` may imply one of four things before it implies a nutrition-analysis application:

1. A seven-meals-per-day bodybuilding program.
2. A weekly meal-planning product.
3. A meal-delivery bundle containing seven meals.
4. A restaurant reward where the seventh meal is free.

The name therefore needs a strong descriptor and visual identity to explain the product immediately.

## Best way to use it

### App Store title

`My Seventh Meal: Food & Macros`

This is exactly 30 characters, matching Apple’s current maximum app-name length.

### Subtitle

`Photo nutrition made clearer`

### Tagline options

- **Understand every meal.**
- **A clearer picture of what you eat.**
- **Photo. Confirm. Track.**
- **Real meals, explained.**

### Possible brand story

The “seventh meal” can represent the meal that finally becomes understandable—the moment when daily food choices, recipes, portions, and habits come together. This story is usable, but it is not obvious from the name alone and would require repeated explanation.

## Final reservation sequence

Before adopting the name:

1. Run live registrar/RDAP checks for the exact `.com`, `.app`, and `get...com` alternatives.
2. Attempt to create `My Seventh Meal` in App Store Connect.
3. Reserve the Android package identifier and verify Google Play title/brand consistency.
4. Complete a comprehensive exact and similar-mark trademark search with counsel.
5. Check company-name and social-handle availability.
6. Test the name blindly against `MealDecode`, `MealFathom`, and one coined alternative with 15–20 target users.
