# Domain 2 — Education / AI Coaching

**Recommendation:** PRIORITY 2  
**Working concept:** Outcome-specific workplace communication and analytics-storytelling coach  
**Initial wedge:** Professionals practice meetings, presentations, executive explanations, difficult conversations, and data storytelling with AI feedback.

## Why this domain is attractive

Education is a proven recurring-subscription category. Duolingo reported **$298.5M of revenue in Q2 2026** and 58.7M daily active users. RevenueCat's 2026 subscription benchmarks also show Education as a meaningful subscription category.

The opportunity for a new entrant is not another broad language-learning catalog. A more attractive wedge is a **high-value professional outcome** where users can justify paying because improvement can affect interviews, presentations, promotion, client conversations, or job performance.

## Customer problem hypothesis

Potential segments:

- Non-native English-speaking professionals.
- Analysts and managers who struggle to translate numbers into executive narratives.
- International job candidates.
- First-time managers practicing feedback and difficult conversations.
- Consultants/sales professionals practicing client presentations.

## Proposed differentiated product

- Profession/seniority/scenario selection.
- Scenario simulator.
- Voice or text response.
- Transcription and rubric-based scoring.
- Feedback on clarity, concision, structure, vocabulary, delivery, confidence, and factual reasoning.
- Improved answer generation preserving user intent.
- Personalized weakness profile.
- Daily practice plan and progress tracking.

### Strongest initial niche

**Analytics and executive communication coach:** practice explaining dashboards, KPI changes, recommendations, and risks to executives.

## AI/audio unit economics

Recommended MVP is asynchronous voice rather than unlimited realtime conversation.

Example active subscriber:

- 20 sessions/month.
- 5 minutes of speech/session = 100 minutes/month.
- Transcription ~ $1.70/month at the referenced rate.
- LLM feedback roughly $0.28/month under a lightweight model assumption.
- TTS allowance roughly $0.25-$1/month.

Planning variable AI/audio cost: **~$2.25-$4/user/month**.

Potential subscription pricing: **$14.99-$24.99/month**, with realtime voice as a capped premium feature.

## Risks

- Generic AI voice tools can replicate superficial functionality.
- Users may try it but fail to build a habit.
- Subjective scoring can feel arbitrary.
- Content quality is a hidden ongoing cost.
- Realtime voice can compress margins.

## Validation

Recruit 15 analysts/managers, give each five realistic scenarios, provide rubric-based feedback, measure repeat practice and improvement, then test $14.99/$19.99/$29.99 pricing.

**Go signal:** at least 30% voluntarily complete 5+ practices in two weeks and 3-5 agree to pay.
