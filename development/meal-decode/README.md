## Product promise

> Help people understand home-cooked and mixed meals through a photo, a few simple confirmation questions, and an honest nutrition estimate shown as a range rather than false precision.

## Core experience

1. Open the app and see a calm daily nutrition summary.
2. Tap **Log a meal**.
3. Take or select a meal photo.
4. Confirm the foods the app thinks it sees.
5. Adjust portions.
6. Answer only the questions that materially change the estimate, such as oil, cream, cooking method, and homemade versus restaurant-prepared.
7. Review a calorie and macro range with a plain-language confidence explanation.
8. Save the meal or remember it as a household recipe.

## Screens currently included

- Welcome and lightweight onboarding
- Home dashboard

- Analysis/loading state
- Nutrition result with estimate explanation

- Weekly progress
- Profile and privacy controls
- Empty states and error/retry state

## UX principles

- Friendly, calm, and nonjudgmental
- Mobile-first for iPhone and popular Android phone sizes
- Large touch targets and readable text
- No forced account creation in the prototype
- No false precision
- No medical diagnosis or treatment language
- Clear privacy choices
- AI proposes; the user confirms; structured nutrition data calculates

## What is real today

- All major screens and navigation are clickable.
- The full meal-logging journey works with sample photos and mock results.
- Meals and recipes can be added during the current browser session.
- Loading, empty, saved, and error states are represented.

## Next design decisions

1. Test the complete logging flow with target users.
2. Measure whether people understand calorie ranges and confidence labels.
3. Reduce any unnecessary confirmation questions.
4. Decide the final brand name.
5. Confirm whether the home screen should lead with calorie range, protein habits, or a more flexible goal-based view.
6. Decide which features belong in the first paid version versus later releases.

## Prototype success criteria

- A new user understands the app's purpose without explanation.
- The meal flow can be completed in approximately 45 seconds.
- Users understand why the estimate is a range.
- Users can explain what the confidence label means.
- Users perceive recipe memory as meaningfully faster than repeated manual logging.
- The design feels trustworthy without looking clinical.
